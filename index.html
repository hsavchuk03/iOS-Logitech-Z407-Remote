iOS Logitech Z407 Remote

Control your Logitech Z407 speakers — volume, bass, and playback — straight from your iPhone over Bluetooth Low Energy.

Download latest release View on GitHub

Latest: v1.0.0 — Initial Release

What it does

  • Scans for and connects to the Z407 over BLE
  • Volume up / down
  • Bass up / down
  • Confirmed working over the AUX input

Known limitations

  • Play/Pause, input switching, and Next/Previous Track are wired up, but their effect depends on the speaker's active input.
  • Over AUX (no track metadata), Play/Pause mutes the speaker and Next/Previous Track are no-ops — this is expected, not a bug.
  • Untested over Bluetooth/USB inputs.

Installation

Option 1 — SideStore

Install Z407Remote.ipa directly through SideStore, which re-signs it with your free Apple ID. This uses one of the 3 app slots a free Apple ID gets, and SideStore re-signs apps roughly every 7 days to keep them working.

Option 2 — LiveContainer (skip using an app slot)

If you'd rather not spend one of your 3 free-Apple-ID app slots, install Z407Remote.ipa inside LiveContainer instead — it runs sideloaded IPAs inside a single container app, so it doesn't count against your slot limit. Commonly paired with SideStore.
